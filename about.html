About This Guide

Welcome to Bacolod Coffee Spots! This guide was built as a passion project to highlight the best independent specialty coffee shops, study hubs, and homegrown roasters around Bacolod City.

Whether you are looking for a quiet nook with reliable Wi-Fi for studying, or a specialty roastery serving locally harvested beans, this guide helps you find your next caffeine stop.
