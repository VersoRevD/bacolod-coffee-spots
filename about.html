Vincent Verso

Hi, I'm Vincent! As a student based in Bacolod, I spend a lot of time searching for high-quality espresso, reliable Wi-Fi, and study-friendly cafes around Lacson Street and nearby areas.

Why I Created This Guide

Finding a comfortable place to study or unwind with a great cup of coffee can be challenging. I created Bacolod Coffee Spots to help fellow students and visitors navigate Bacolod City's booming cafe scene without wasting time or money on subpar spots.

Why You Can Trust These Recommendations

Every place featured in this guide has been personally visited and evaluated. I test each spot based on coffee craft quality, atmosphere, outlet availability, and student-friendliness. None of these listings are sponsored—just genuine recommendations from a local resident.
