About This Spot

Origami Coffee Co. is a premier Japanese-inspired specialty coffee shop located along Lacson Street. Known for its precise pour-over brewing methods and minimalist aesthetic, it provides an ideal environment for focused study sessions, reading, or quiet conversations. They serve single-origin beans sourced from local and international roasters, paired with light Japanese pastries.
